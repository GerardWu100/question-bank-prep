## Macaulay duration
The weighted average time until a bond's cash flows arrive. The weight of each cash flow is its share of the bond price.

## Modified duration
The approximate percentage price change for a 1 percentage point change in yield:

$$\%\Delta P \approx -D_{mod} \times \Delta y$$

## Worked example
A bond with modified duration 7 and a yield rise of 0.50 percentage points: $\%\Delta P \approx -7 \times 0.005 = -3.5\%$.
