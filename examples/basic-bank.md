# Chapter 1 Time Value of Money

## Question 1
An investor deposits \$1,000 at 5% annual interest, compounded once a year. What is the balance after two years?

### Choices
A. \$1,100.00
B. \$1,102.50
C. \$1,105.00

### Answer
B

### Solution
Future value: $FV = PV \times (1 + r)^n = 1000 \times 1.05^2 = 1102.50$.

## Question 2
Which change **increases** the present value of a fixed future cash flow?

### Choices
A. A higher discount rate
B. A longer time until payment
C. A lower discount rate

### Answer
C

### Solution
$PV = FV / (1 + r)^n$. A smaller $r$ makes the denominator smaller, so $PV$ rises.

# Chapter 2 Statistics

## Question 1
A portfolio returned 10%, then -10%. What is the geometric mean return per period?

### Choices
- A. 0.00%
- B. -0.50%
- C. -1.00%

### Answer
**B**

### Solution
$\sqrt{1.10 \times 0.90} - 1 = \sqrt{0.99} - 1 \approx -0.50\%$.

## Question 2
Which measure of spread uses every observation?

### Choices
A. Range
B. Interquartile range
C. Standard deviation

### Answer
Answer: C

### Explanation
Standard deviation uses each observation's distance from the mean. The range uses only the two extremes.
